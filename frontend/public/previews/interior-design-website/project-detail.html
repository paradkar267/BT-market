The Waverly Residence

Tribeca, New York

Location Tribeca, Manhattan
Type Full Renovation
Size 3,200 sq ft
Timeline 14 Months
Service Full-Service Design

The Brief

A young family sought to transform a raw Tribeca loft into a warm, functional home that could grow with their two children. The 3,200-square-foot space presented both an opportunity and a challenge: how to create intimacy and warmth within the expansive, industrial bones of a cast-iron building.

Our Approach

We leaned into the contrast — preserving the original columns, exposed brick, and soaring ceilings while introducing warm walnut millwork, natural stone surfaces, and a material palette inspired by the family's summers in the south of France. Every material was chosen to age gracefully, developing character rather than showing wear.
