Our Financial Principles

100% Trade Discount Transparency

We pass all trade discounts on furnishings, fabrics, and materials directly to you with zero retail markup. Our compensation comes solely from our agreed-upon design fee.

Milestone Billing

Payments are broken into transparent milestones tied to real progress: 30% initial deposit, 30% design approval, 30% procurement completion, and 10% post-installation walk-through.

Guaranteed Budget Bands

During the concept phase, we establish precise target spending bands for furniture and construction. We do not specify items that exceed your approved budget envelope.
