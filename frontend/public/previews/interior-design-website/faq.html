General

Our studio is in SoHo, Manhattan at 147 Mercer Street. We work with clients across the greater New York area, including Connecticut, New Jersey, and the Hudson Valley. For select projects, we also work nationally.

We are a deliberately small studio of four senior professionals. Every client works directly with our principal designer, Eleanor Voss, supported by our full team. We limit the number of active projects to ensure each receives our complete attention.

Yes. While we prefer to begin with an in-person site visit, our initial consultations can be conducted virtually. We've successfully completed projects with clients we first met over video call.

Pricing & Investment

Our projects typically range from $50,000 to $500,000+ in design fees, depending on scope and complexity. We provide detailed, fixed-fee proposals after an initial consultation, so there are no surprises. Material and furnishing budgets are separate and scaled to your investment comfort zone.

We use milestone-based billing tied to project phases: concept approval, design development completion, sourcing completion, and installation. This keeps costs predictable and tied to tangible progress.

For full-service engagements, we maintain a minimum design fee of $50,000. For standalone consultations, our fee is $2,500 for a half-day session.

Process & Timeline

Furnishing-only projects typically run 3–6 months. Full-service renovations range from 6–18 months depending on scope and complexity. We provide a detailed timeline during the design phase and update it regularly throughout the project.

Minor refinements are a natural part of the process and included in our scope. Significant scope changes after design approval may require a change order with adjusted fees and timeline — we'll discuss this transparently before proceeding.

We provide full project management through installation, coordinating with your contractors, craftspeople, and vendors. While we don't perform construction ourselves, we oversee every detail to ensure the design vision is executed faithfully.
