How We Work

A clear path from
vision to reality.

We've refined our process over 14 years and 120+ projects to eliminate the uncertainty that makes renovation stressful. Every engagement follows a transparent five-phase structure designed to protect your investment.

5-Phase Structure Milestone Billing 94% On-Time Completion
01

Discovery

We begin with listening. An in-depth consultation to understand your lifestyle, aesthetic sensibilities, functional requirements, and investment comfort zone.

02

Design

Concepts take shape through moodboards, floor plans, and 3D visualizations. We present options, refine together, and finalize every detail before anything is ordered.

03

Sourcing

We draw from a curated network of artisans, manufacturers, and trade resources worldwide. Every material is specified, sampled, and approved before procurement.

04

Installation

Our project management team coordinates every delivery, every contractor, every detail. You receive regular updates without needing to manage anything.

05

Reveal

The moment it all comes together. We handle the final styling, walk you through your completed space, and make sure every element is exactly right.

Why our clients stay calm.

Interior design has a reputation for blown budgets and missed deadlines. Our structured process exists to prevent exactly that. Fixed-fee proposals, milestone-based billing, weekly progress reports, and a dedicated project manager for every engagement. We've completed 94% of our projects on time and on budget — and the other 6% came in under.
