The Founder

Eleanor Voss

After two decades at the intersection of architecture and fine art, Eleanor founded The Atelier in 2012 with a simple conviction: great interior design should feel inevitable, not imposed. Trained at the Rhode Island School of Design and honed through years at Skidmore, Owings & Merrill, she brings an architectural rigor to residential work that most decorators lack — and a warmth that most architects forget.

The studio name reflects the philosophy: an atelier is a workshop where craft meets vision, where every project receives the focused attention of a single, dedicated team.

Material Honesty

We let materials speak in their own voice. Stone remains stone, wood remains wood — no veneers, no imitations, no shortcuts.

02

Lived-In Elegance

A room should look better after five years of life than it did on installation day. We design for patina, not perfection.

03

Quiet Confidence

The best design doesn't announce itself. It creates a feeling — calm, warmth, rightness — that you sense before you can name.
